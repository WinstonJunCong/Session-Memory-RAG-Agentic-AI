# Ticket Management Guide

## What is a ticket?
A ticket is a record of a customer's support request. Every inbound message from a connected channel automatically creates a ticket in NovaDesk.

## Ticket Statuses
| Status | Description |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Ticket is active and awaiting a response |
| **Pending** | Waiting for a reply from the customer |
| **Resolved** | Issue has been resolved |
| **Closed** | Ticket is archived and no longer active |

## How do I assign a ticket to an agent?
Open the ticket and click the **Assignee** field on the right panel. Select an agent from the dropdown. The agent will receive an email notification.

## How do I set ticket priority?
Ticket priority can be set to Low, Medium, High, or Urgent. To set priority, open the ticket and click the **Priority** field in the right panel. Urgent tickets appear highlighted in red on the dashboard.

## Can I merge duplicate tickets?
Yes. Open one of the tickets, click the **More Options** (⋯) menu, and select **Merge Ticket**. Search for the duplicate ticket by ID or customer email and confirm the merge. The older ticket will be closed.

## What are ticket tags?
Tags are labels you can apply to tickets for filtering and reporting. Examples include `billing`, `bug`, `feature-request`, and `urgent`. You can manage tags from **Settings > Tags**.

## How do I reopen a resolved ticket?
Open the resolved ticket and click **Reopen**. The ticket status will change back to Open and it will reappear in the active ticket queue.
