# Billing & Subscription FAQ

## What payment methods does NovaDesk accept?
NovaDesk accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. We do not currently accept cryptocurrency or bank transfers for standard subscriptions.

## How do I upgrade my plan?
To upgrade your plan, navigate to **Settings > Billing > Change Plan**. Changes take effect immediately and you will be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of your billing cycle.

## Can I get a refund?
NovaDesk offers a **14-day money-back guarantee** for new subscriptions. After 14 days, refunds are issued only in cases of billing errors. To request a refund, contact support@novadesk.io with your order ID.

## What happens if my payment fails?
If a payment fails, NovaDesk will retry the charge after 3 days and again after 7 days. If all retries fail, your account will be downgraded to the Free plan and your data will be retained for 30 days.

## How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel anytime from **Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription**. Your plan remains active until the end of the current billing period. No partial refunds are issued for unused days.

## Do you offer annual billing discounts?
Yes. Annual billing offers a **20% discount** compared to monthly billing. You can switch to annual billing from the Billing settings page at any time.
