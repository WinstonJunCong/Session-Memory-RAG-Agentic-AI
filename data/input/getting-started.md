# Getting Started with NovaDesk

## What is NovaDesk?
NovaDesk is a customer support platform that helps teams manage tickets, automate responses, and track customer satisfaction — all in one place.

## Step 1: Create Your Workspace
After signing up, you will be prompted to create a **Workspace**. Your workspace is your team's shared environment. Give it a name that matches your company or team.

## Step 2: Invite Your Team
Go to **Settings > Team Members > Invite**. Enter your colleagues' email addresses and assign them one of the following roles:
- **Admin** – Full access to all settings and data
- **Agent** – Can manage and respond to tickets
- **Viewer** – Read-only access to tickets and reports

## Step 3: Connect Your Support Channels
NovaDesk supports the following channels:
- **Email** – Connect a support email (e.g. support@yourcompany.com)
- **Live Chat** – Embed the NovaDesk chat widget on your website
- **WhatsApp** – Link your WhatsApp Business account

Go to **Settings > Channels** to connect each channel.

## Step 4: Create Your First Ticket
Tickets are created automatically when a customer contacts you. You can also create tickets manually by clicking **New Ticket** from the dashboard.

## Step 5: Set Up Automations
Use **Automations** to auto-assign tickets, send canned responses, or escalate urgent issues. Go to **Settings > Automations > New Rule** to get started.
